# MTurk 2020 questionnaire text

Text extracted from the original questionnaire. Block labels and wording are preserved. Decorative icons and the unrelated HIT-count illustration are omitted; page-break labels retain the recorded order. The original files remain in the authors’ archive.

Political perceptions of economic conditions

Start of Block: Consent screen

Q1 This survey is designed to better understand Americans' political preferences and their views on the economy. It will take about 15 minutes. There are about 40 (mostly multiple choice) questions you are expected to answer. If you come across a question you are not comfortable answering, you may simply skip it.
 
Your responses will be kept completely confidential. The data that we collect will be stored at a secure location and no identifying information about you will be made public. Only statistical summaries of the responses will be presented in research journals or academic conferences. 

You will receive $0.75 for completing the survey, paid through Amazon Mechanical Turk. 

To participate in the study, you must be at least 18 years old and be a United States resident.

If you have any questions about the survey, please contact Dr. Carolyn Roush at croush (at) fsu (dot) edu.
